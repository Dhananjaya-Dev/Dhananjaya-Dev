### 🌌 Overview & Vision
Traditional blood banks frequently face challenges due to manual record-keeping and delayed operations. To overcome these limitations, this Full-Stack Intelligent Blood Bank Management System was developed, offering automated donor management, real-time inventory tracking, and AI-driven predictive insights to improve efficiency, accuracy, and healthcare decision-making.
